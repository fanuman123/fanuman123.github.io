Home Assistant

A private connection between the owner's Google Nest devices and Home Assistant.

What this connection does

This personal application connects the owner's Nest thermostat, indoor camera and doorbell to their own Home Assistant installation. It supports thermostat monitoring and control, camera viewing, and device events, according to the permissions granted through Google.

It is intended for the owner's household. It is not a public service and is not an official Google or Home Assistant website.

Your devices stay private

This website contains information only. It provides no login, device controls, camera images, household readings, or access to Home Assistant.

For support, use the support email displayed on this application's Google consent screen.
