Scope

This notice describes the owner's personal Google Nest integration with their private Home Assistant installation. It is intended for the owner's household, rather than the general public.

Google data accessed and its purpose

Depending on the devices and permissions authorised, the integration accesses device identifiers and names, thermostat settings, temperature and humidity, heating status, camera or doorbell streams and event media, and motion or doorbell events. It sends thermostat commands when requested by the owner or their configured household automations.

These functions provide household monitoring and device control. Google authorisation credentials are used to maintain the authorised connection.

Storage and retention

Integration configuration, credentials, device states and recorded history are held by the owner's Home Assistant installation and its configured backups. Event images or recordings may be retained according to the owner's Home Assistant and Google settings. Retention and deletion follow those configurations.

Sharing and protection

The public information website does not receive Nest data or credentials. The integration communicates with Google's services and the owner's private Home Assistant installation. Access within the household and any additional integrations are controlled by the owner. This application does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising or model training.

Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can revoke this application's access through their Google account permissions and remove the Nest integration from Home Assistant. Revocation stops future authorised access; previously retained history, media and backups must be deleted separately from the systems holding them.
